Privacy policy

Karta zhizni is a personal, single-user application. Its only user is its owner.

What data is accessed

Read-only access, granted by the owner through Google OAuth, to the owner's Google Health data: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep.

How it is used

The data is summarized per day and shown only in the owner's private dashboard. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models.

Storage and deletion

Daily summaries are stored in the owner's private dashboard storage. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections; the owner can delete the stored summaries at any time.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
